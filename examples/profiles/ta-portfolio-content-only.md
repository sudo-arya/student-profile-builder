# About Me

I am a doctoral researcher working at the intersection of **trustworthy machine learning**,
natural language processing, and human-centred computing. My research explores how intelligent
systems can remain reliable, understandable, and useful in high-impact settings.

I am currently advised by [Professor R. Sharma](https://example.com) and collaborate with the
Responsible Computing Lab. Before beginning my doctorate, I worked on applied data-science
projects in healthcare and education.

> My goal is to build AI systems that people can understand, question, and confidently use.

# Current Research

## Reliable language models for public services

This project studies uncertainty estimation, factual consistency, and human oversight for
language models used in information-access systems.

**Methods:** retrieval-augmented generation, calibration, evaluation design, and participatory
user studies.

## Explainable decision support

We are developing explanation methods that adapt to the needs of domain experts and affected
users rather than providing a single technical explanation for everyone.

# Featured Projects

## Bharosa — Model reliability toolkit

An open-source toolkit for measuring calibration, subgroup performance, and failure patterns
in classification and language-model pipelines.

**Technologies:** Python, PyTorch, FastAPI, and Docker  
[Source code](https://github.com/aarya-mehta-demo/bharosa) · [Project page](https://example.com/bharosa)

## Samvaad — Multilingual research assistant

A research prototype for retrieving and summarizing public documents across Indian languages,

**Technologies:** Python, vector search, JavaScript, and accessibility testing
